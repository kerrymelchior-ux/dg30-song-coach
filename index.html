Connect your piano

Use one option. Bluetooth MIDI is the most accurate: the piano tells the app exactly which keys you press, including chords.

Bluetooth MIDI (recommended) On the DG30, make sure Bluetooth MIDI is on. Close the Kawai PianoRemote app first, since only one app can connect at a time.
Don't pair the piano in your phone's Bluetooth settings for this. That pairing is for music audio. The app connects to the piano's MIDI itself when you tap Find my piano.
USB cable or already-paired MIDI For a USB-C to USB-B cable into the piano's USB to Host port.
Phone microphone Backup option. Hears one note at a time and can mistake octaves, so it is forgiving about octave.

Read sheet music

Add the pages in any order; they're sorted by the page numbers printed on them. Claude reads each page and writes out every note. Pages are read two at a time; a busy page can take a minute or two. Keep each page flat, well lit and square to the camera, with the whole page in frame.

Needed once. It stays on this phone and is sent only to Anthropic. Get one at console.anthropic.com; each page usually costs a few cents.
