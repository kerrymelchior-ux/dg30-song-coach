Add a song

Pick an audio file (MP3, WAV, M4A) and the app turns it into notes on your phone. You can also add MIDI or MusicXML files, several at once with Add several MIDI files. Got a song file from someone with Song Coach? Tap Add a shared song file: it's ready to play straight away, no scanning or API key needed. For Suno songs, download the piano or melody stem; full-band mixes convert poorly.

Backup and other devices

A backup is one file with all your songs and playlists (not your API key). Keep it on your phone or in Google Drive, then open the app on your other device and tap Restore.
Looks up a cover for every song that has an artist but no picture yet. You can change any of them with Edit.
Fix storage
  1. Tap Save backup to my phone above first, so nothing is lost.
  2. In Chrome, tap the icon left of the web address → Permissions (or Site settings). Make sure On-device site data (or Cookies and site data) is Allowed.
  3. If it already is, tap Delete data / Clear & reset for this site there. That repairs Chrome's damaged storage for the app.
  4. Reopen the app, then Restore from a backup and pick the file you saved.

How it works

Wait stops the notes until you press the right keys on the piano.
Play keeps time and scores each note you hit within a quarter second.
Listen plays the song on your piano so you can hear it first.
Aa switches a song with words between falling notes and big words with the chords above them. The keyboard still lights the keys to play.
Key − and + move the whole song down or up a half step, for an easier key or a singer's range.
Songs with chord symbols get a Guitar button: chords scroll down with finger positions on the neck, and Listen strums them.
Turn your phone sideways for the widest keyboard.

Connect your piano

Use one option. Bluetooth MIDI is the most accurate: the piano tells the app exactly which keys you press, including chords.

Bluetooth MIDI (recommended) On your piano, make sure Bluetooth MIDI is on. Close any other piano app first (like Kawai PianoRemote), since only one app can connect at a time.
Don't pair the piano in your phone's Bluetooth settings for this. That pairing is for music audio. The app connects to the piano's MIDI itself when you tap Find my piano.
USB cable or already-paired MIDI For a USB-C to USB-B cable into the piano's USB to Host port.
Phone microphone Backup option. Hears one note at a time and can mistake octaves, so it is forgiving about octave.

Read sheet music

Add the pages in any order; they're sorted by their page numbers. Claude reads the notes, the words and the tempo marking on each page. A busy page can take a minute or two. Keep pages flat, well lit and fully in frame.

Reading sheet music needs an Anthropic API key.
